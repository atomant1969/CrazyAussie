## Purpose

This curriculum turns the creation of this project plan into tutorial content for other YouTubers.

The point is to teach beginners that a YouTube channel is not just recording random videos. It can be planned like a real project:

* purpose
* audience
* content pillars
* video roadmap
* checklist
* workflow
* monetization plan
* navigation/index
* review process

## Core Message

Before you make 100 disconnected videos, build a simple project plan for your channel.

A plan does not need to be corporate or complicated. It just needs to answer:

1. Who is this channel for?
2. What problem does it solve?
3. What videos come first?
4. What does the channel become later?
5. How will progress be tracked?
6. How will the creator avoid losing ideas and momentum?

## Planning Areas

### 1. Why Plan A YouTube Channel

Teach why random uploads make growth harder, why decisions should be written down, and how planning reduces decision fatigue.

Video ideas:

### 2. Define The Channel Promise

Teach the formula:

```text
This channel helps [viewer] do/understand [result] by [method/style].
```

Example:

```text
This channel helps practical beginners learn how to make YouTube videos while building toward a real Siberian homestead documentary.
```

Video ideas:

* Write Your YouTube Channel Promise in One Sentence
* Who Is Your YouTube Channel Actually For?
* Stop Saying Your Channel Is About Everything

### 3. Choose Starting Audience And Future Audience

Teach that some channels start with one helpful audience and later transition into the larger project.

For this project:

* Starting audience: new YouTubers who need beginner workflow help.
* Future audience: viewers interested in the Siberian homestead documentary.

Video ideas:

* Starting Audience vs Future Audience on YouTube
* How to Grow Before Your Main Project Is Ready
* How to Bridge One Content Type Into Another

### 4. Build Content Pillars

Teach that content pillars are the repeatable categories the channel returns to.

Example:

1. Beginner YouTube workflow.
2. Editing software and tutorials.
3. Uploading and publishing.
4. Channel building in public.
5. Monetization education.
6. Siberian homestead documentary.

Video ideas:

* How to Choose Content Pillars for a YouTube Channel

### 9. Choose Direct AdSense Or MCN

Teach that monetization route choice is a planning decision, not something to improvise after hitting eligibility.

Note: if someone says "MCP" in this context, clarify whether they mean an **MCN**, a **managed CMS**, or another payout partner. The public lesson should use accurate terms.

#### Direct AdSense Route

Good fit when:

* the creator's country is supported
* identity documents match the account country
* address verification is possible
* tax forms can be submitted honestly
* bank payout is straightforward
* the creator wants to avoid network fees

Pros:

* no MCN percentage cut
* direct relationship with YouTube/Google
* more control
* no network lock-in

Risks:

* country, ID, address, tax, and bank setup must line up
* mistakes can delay or block payment
* some creators may not have a clean direct route

#### MCN / Managed CMS Route

Good fit when:

* direct AdSense is difficult or impossible
* the creator needs alternative payout routing
* the creator wants network support
* the MCN can legally and practically solve a real problem

Pros:

* may solve payout complications
* may support managed CMS linking
* may provide support, Content ID, or other tools
* may be simpler for creators in complicated regions

Risks:

* network fee
* contract lock-in
* bad support
* unclear exit terms
* possible loss of control

#### Decision Framework

Teach viewers to compare:

| Question | Direct AdSense | MCN / Managed CMS |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Can I verify identity, address, tax, and bank details cleanly? | Stronger if yes | Useful if no |
| Do I need special payout routing? | Usually weaker | Often stronger |
| Do I want maximum control? | Stronger | Weaker |
| Am I willing to pay a fee for support/routing? | No | Yes |
| Can I leave easily? | Usually yes | Depends on contract |
| Do I understand tax withholding separately from fees? | Required | Still required |

#### Beginner Mistakes

* Thinking an MCN bypasses YouTube rules.
* Thinking direct AdSense is always better.
* Thinking MCN is always safer.
* Confusing MCN fee with tax withholding.
* Confusing tax treaty rates with YouTube commission.
* Signing a contract before asking how to leave.
* Choosing a country/payment route they cannot document.
