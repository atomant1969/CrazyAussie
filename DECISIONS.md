## Current Decisions

* **Final channel name**: Not locked yet.
* **Working name**: The Crazy Aussie in Siberia.
* **Style on camera**: Mixed. Use face, voiceover, and tutorial/screen formats as needed.
* **Early channel angle**: Equal mix of helping new YouTubers and building the channel in public.
* **Phase-one business target**: Earn $20,000 USD total creator revenue to buy land for the off-grid Siberian project.
* **50k subscriber target**: Desired milestone, but not necessarily a hard monetization rule.
* **YPP timing preference**: Wait until 50k subscribers before applying, unless this decision is revisited.
* **50k target timeframe**: 3 months.
* **Early lead-generation focus**: Editing software reviews and tutorials.
* **Editing app tutorials**: Major pillar.
* **Workflow taught**: Computer-based workflow first.
* **Main recording device for now**: Android phone.
* **Target beginner type**: Builders and homesteaders who want to learn YouTube.
* **Target region**: Broad English-speaking global audience.
* **Language**: English only for now.
* **Siberia/build footage timing**: Expected in 3-12 months.
* **Existing Siberia visuals**: None yet.
* **Personal backstory level**: Minimal.
* **Current status**: Already in Siberia.
* **First build task**: Unknown.
* **Land type**: Forest land.
* **Build labour**: Mostly self plus family/friends.
* **Build content style**: Balanced technical and documentary style.
* **Lead-generation video length**: Shorts-led at first, with 10-minute tutorial/review videos where useful.
* **Later documentary length**: Up to 1 hour for major build episodes.
* **Shorts output**: About 2 Shorts per week to start.
* **Script style**: Mix of scripts, bullet outlines, and shot plans depending on video type.
* **Voiceover**: Prefer not for now.
* **Talking to camera**: Willing to learn.
* **Monetization route assumption**: MCN is the likely route.
* **Monetization content**: Public from the early stage.
* **Sensitive topics**: Avoid politics, sanctions, banking details, family/private life, and location specifics.
* **Humor level**: Serious with dry Aussie humor, with room for balanced personality.
* **Land privacy**: Keep exact location fully private.
* **Planning system**: This repo is the master plan.
* **File structure**: Use both an overview plan and separate working files.
* **Format preference**: Markdown checklists.
