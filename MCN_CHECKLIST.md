# MCN Checklist

Parent: [Monetization Curriculum](MONETIZATION_CURRICULUM.md)

Related: [Master Checklist](CHECKLIST.md)

## Assumption

The likely monetization route is an MCN, because direct AdSense setup may be difficult depending on country, ID, address, banking, and regional restrictions.

An MCN fee is separate from YouTube's platform revenue share and separate from U.S. withholding tax. Keep these separate in all public explanations.

## Timing

Do not sign with an MCN just because the channel becomes eligible. Research early, but sign only when the channel has leverage and the payout route is understood.

Current preference: wait until around **50,000 subscribers** before applying for monetization or linking to an MCN, unless the strategy changes.

## Research Checklist

- [ ] Build a shortlist of MCNs.
- [ ] Confirm each MCN is active and reputable.
- [ ] Confirm whether each MCN supports creators filming from Russia.
- [ ] Confirm whether each MCN can pay to a Kazakhstan bank account.
- [ ] Confirm what ID documents are required from an Australian citizen.
- [ ] Confirm whether a personal AdSense account is required.
- [ ] Confirm whether the MCN uses a managed CMS setup.
- [ ] Confirm fee percentage.
- [ ] Confirm whether the fee is taken from gross or net revenue.
- [ ] Confirm minimum payout amount.
- [ ] Confirm payout schedule.
- [ ] Confirm contract term.
- [ ] Confirm exit notice period.
- [ ] Confirm whether there are penalties for leaving.
- [ ] Confirm whether the agreement is exclusive.
- [ ] Confirm who controls Content ID.
- [ ] Confirm whether sponsorships and affiliate income stay outside the MCN.
- [ ] Confirm what support is actually provided.
- [ ] Search for creator complaints before signing.
- [ ] Get the contract reviewed before signing if the channel has meaningful revenue potential.

## Questions To Ask An MCN

1. Can you monetize a channel filmed from Russia without a personal Russian AdSense account?
2. Can you pay an Australian citizen into a Kazakhstan bank account?
3. What documents do you need from me?
4. Do you require my channel to be linked into your managed CMS?
5. What percentage do you take?
6. Is the percentage taken from gross YouTube revenue or from revenue after YouTube's share?
7. What is the contract length?
8. How do I leave the MCN?
9. Are there penalties or delays when leaving?
10. Do you control Content ID or copyright claims?
11. Do you take any percentage of sponsorships, affiliate links, donations, or merch?
12. What happens if YouTube changes monetization requirements?
13. What happens if payout routes change?
14. Who is responsible for U.S. tax forms and withholding?
15. Do you provide tax forms or payout statements that show withholding and fees separately?
16. Is your fee deducted before or after any tax withholding?
