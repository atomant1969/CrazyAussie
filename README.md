## YouTube Channel Project Plan

The Crazy Aussie in Siberia is a YouTube channel project that starts with editing software reviews and tutorials for new YouTubers, then gradually transitions into the long-term documentary story: an Australian building a life and homestead in Siberia.

The first business target is to earn **$20,000 USD total creator revenue** so the land can be bought for the off-grid main part of the channel.

### Channel Positioning

* **Core promise**: Help practical beginners learn YouTube editing while building toward a real Siberian homestead documentary.
* **Starting audience**: Builders, homesteaders, practical creators, and new YouTubers who need editing tutorials and software reviews.
* **Long-term audience**: English-speaking viewers interested in off-grid building, Siberia, rural construction, tools, and self-reliance.
* **Tone**: Serious, practical, observant, and honest, with dry Aussie humor where it fits.
* **Format mix**: Editing reviews/tutorials, Shorts, channel-building-in-public updates, then later documentary build episodes.

### Strategic Hook

The channel is built around three tensions:

1. **Creator growth before the build**: Build the audience by helping people make better YouTube videos.
2. **The $20,000 land fund**: Teach useful YouTube skills while funding the land needed for the off-grid project.
3. **Aussie vs. Siberia**: A foreigner adapting to Siberian rural life and climate.
4. **Dual-climate engineering**: Later structures must work across intense summer heat and extreme winter cold.

### Content Pillars

1. **Editing software reviews and tutorials**: Searchable lead-generation videos for new YouTubers.
2. **Channel building in public**: The 3-month 50k subscriber attempt and lessons learned.
3. **Siberian project preparation**: Filming plans, privacy boundaries, build roadmap, and project setup.
4. **Homestead build documentary**: Land clearing, first structure, banya, cabin, house, tools, materials, and climate challenges.
5. **Monetization and operations**: YPP milestones, MCN research, and public high-level monetization planning.

### Lead Generation Strategy

Early videos should attract beginner creators through editing software reviews and tutorials. The Siberian project should be present as the channel identity and future destination, but the tutorials must still work as useful standalone videos.

Examples:

* Best free editing software for new YouTubers.
* CapCut Desktop review for beginners.
* DaVinci Resolve review for beginners.
* How to make your first YouTube edit.
* How to export a YouTube video correctly.

The bridge is important: mention the larger Siberian project naturally, but do not force it into every editing tutorial before real project footage exists.

### Production Pipeline

1. **Idea**: Capture hook, audience, title candidates, and thumbnail concept.
2. **Outline**: Write the first 30 seconds, key beats, payoff, and call to action.
3. **Record**: Capture Android phone A-roll, screen recordings, tutorial examples, and backup audio where possible.
4. **Edit**: Cut for pace, add captions, tighten dead space, and build the first 15 seconds last.
5. **Package**: Finalize title, thumbnail, description, chapters, Shorts, and pinned comment.
6. **Review**: Track retention, click-through rate, comments, and ideas for follow-up videos.

### Upload Rhythm

* **Main video**: 1 tutorial/review per week minimum.
* **Shorts**: About 2 per week to start.
* **Community post**: 1 poll or question per week.
* **Monthly review**: Pick the best-performing topic and turn it into a series.

### Build Seasons

1. **Season 1: Land and First Task**: Access, clearing, privacy-safe setup, and deciding the first structure.
2. **Season 2: First Structure**: Shed, banya, cabin, or another practical first build depending on conditions.
3. **Season 3: Winter Cabin**: Small insulated living base for longer stays and winter work.
4. **Season 4: Main House**: Foundation, framing, insulation, services, and permanent living systems.

### Monetization Assumptions

* The channel should target broad English-speaking global traffic.
* Direct AdSense setup may be difficult if the required identity, country, bank, and address documents do not line up.
* An MCN is the likely monetization route unless research changes that assumption.
* Any MCN should be treated as a business contract: check payout routes, fees, term length, exit clauses, Content ID rules, and support quality before signing.
* Public monetization content should stay high-level and avoid politics, sanctions mechanics, banking details, family/private life, and exact location.
