## Launch Strategy

Start with content that new YouTubers are already searching for: editing software reviews, editing tutorials, upload workflow, and beginner production guidance.

The channel should not feel like a generic editing channel. Each video should gradually reveal that the creator is preparing to document a real Siberian homestead project.

Every major video should also pass through:

* [Viral Video Strategy](VIRAL_VIDEO_STRATEGY.md): hook, tension, title, thumbnail, and shareability check.
* [Distribution And Promotion Plan](DISTRIBUTION_PROMOTION_PLAN.md): Shorts, TikTok, X.com, Pinterest, Facebook, and external promotion workflow.

### Minimum Android Phone Specs

Recommended baseline for beginners:

* 1080p video recording.
* 30 fps minimum, 60 fps preferred.
* 64 GB storage minimum, 128 GB preferred.
* USB-C file transfer.
* Enough battery to record multiple short takes.
* Rear camera that can lock focus and exposure.
* Clean lens, stable tripod mount, and enough storage space matter more than having the newest phone.

### Thumbnail Basics

Beginner rule: the thumbnail is the image that helps someone decide whether to click the video.

Teach:

* Difference between auto-generated thumbnails and custom thumbnails.
* How to pick the best frame if using an auto-generated thumbnail.
* How to upload a custom thumbnail.
* Why the thumbnail must still look clear when small.
* Why the title and thumbnail should work together.
* How to check the thumbnail on mobile after publishing.

## Shorts Formats

Use Shorts as low-effort growth tests and funnels into longer videos.

* One software feature beginners should know.
* One mistake new YouTubers make.
* One before/after edit.
* One upload checklist item.
* One Siberia project hint or update.
