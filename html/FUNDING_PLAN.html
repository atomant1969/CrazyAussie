Land Funding Plan

Parent: Full Project Plan

Related: Monetization Curriculum | Master Checklist

Purpose

The first career phase of the channel has a concrete business job:

Earn $20,000 USD in total creator revenue so the land can be bought for the off-grid Siberian homestead project.

Subscriber growth matters because it creates reach, trust, and monetization options. But the real phase-one target is not only 50,000 subscribers. The real target is enough verified revenue to purchase the land and begin the main documentary phase.

Strategic Target

  • Funding target: $20,000 USD total.
  • Use of funds: Land purchase for the off-grid main channel project.
  • Audience target before full transition: 50,000 subscribers.
  • Timeframe target: 3 months for the first aggressive growth sprint.
  • Primary content engine: beginner YouTube setup, editing tutorials, upload workflow lessons, channel planning lessons, monetization education, and building-the-channel-in-public updates.

Main Principle

Do not treat subscribers as success by themselves.

The channel can reach a large audience and still fail the land objective if revenue is not tracked, monetization is delayed, or the audience does not trust the project enough to support it.

The project should track:

  • Subscribers.
  • Long-form watch hours.
  • Shorts views.
  • Revenue earned.
  • Revenue source.
  • Net amount after fees, tax withholding, platform share, MCN fee, payment fees, and refunds where relevant.
  • Amount reserved for land.
  • Remaining gap to $20,000 USD.

Revenue Sources To Consider

YouTube Partner Program

Use this only after the payout path is clear.

Potential sources:

  • Watch page ads.
  • Shorts ad revenue.
  • YouTube Premium revenue.
  • Fan funding if available.

Important:

  • Subscribers alone do not create revenue.
  • Long-form watch hours are likely more useful for the later documentary channel than Shorts-only growth.
  • Do not apply through a route that creates identity, bank, country, or contract problems.

MCN Route

An MCN may be practical if direct AdSense is difficult.

Track:

  • MCN percentage.
  • Whether the percentage is taken before or after YouTube's platform share.
  • Payment routes.
  • Contract term.
  • Exit clause.
  • Whether sponsorships, affiliates, donations, and products are outside the MCN cut.

Affiliate Revenue

Good fit for the beginner tutorial phase if recommendations are honest.

Possible categories:

  • Editing software.
  • Creator tools.
  • Microphones.
  • Tripods.
  • Lights.
  • Storage drives.
  • Phone mounts.

Rules:

  • Only recommend tools that make sense for beginners.
  • Disclose affiliate links clearly.
  • Separate honest reviews from sales pressure.

Sponsorships

Possible once the channel has a specific creator audience.

Good sponsor fit:

  • Editing software.
  • Screen recording tools.
  • Cloud backup.
  • Audio tools.
  • Creator hardware.
  • Thumbnail/design tools.

Avoid:

  • Bad-fit sponsors.
  • Sponsors that reduce trust.
  • Sponsorships that require dishonest claims.

Digital Products

This is a natural fit because the project already creates useful planning assets.

Possible products:

  • Beginner YouTube checklist.
  • Video planning template.
  • Upload checklist.
  • Editing review template.
  • Channel project planning pack.
  • Monetization route comparison worksheet.

Rule:

The free videos must remain useful on their own. Paid products should save time or add structure, not hold back essential information.

Consulting Or Coaching

Possible later, but time-intensive.

Good only if:

  • The channel has trust.
  • The offer is narrow.
  • Delivery time does not stop video production.

Content That Supports The Funding Goal

The strongest phase-one videos should do at least one of these:

  1. Attract beginner YouTubers through search.
  2. Build trust by openly documenting the channel plan.
  3. Teach monetization without hype.
  4. Create legitimate affiliate or product opportunities.
  5. Explain the land fund goal honestly without begging.
  6. Bridge viewers from tutorial content into the future Siberian homestead story.

Public Message

Simple version for videos:

The first mission of this channel is to teach practical beginners how to start making YouTube videos. The bigger mission is to earn enough through the channel to buy the land for the off-grid Siberian build. The target is $20,000 USD, and I will show the planning process as we go.

Use the exact revenue numbers publicly only when it is useful, safe, and does not expose private banking or location details.

Risks

Revenue Takes Longer Than Audience Growth

Subscriber growth can happen before meaningful income.

Mitigation:

  • Track revenue separately from subscribers.
  • Build long-form watch hours early.
  • Add affiliate/product options only where they genuinely help viewers.

Trust Damage From Over-Monetizing

Beginners can feel sold to if every tutorial becomes a pitch.

Mitigation:

  • Keep tutorials useful without purchase.
  • Disclose all affiliate/sponsor relationships.
  • Review tools honestly, including negatives.

Wrong Monetization Route

A bad MCN or messy direct AdSense setup can delay payment or reduce revenue.

Mitigation:

  • Research MCNs before signing.
  • Confirm payout paths before applying.
  • Keep country, tax, and payment claims factual and documentable.

Decision Gate Before Land Purchase

Before moving fully into the land/build documentary phase, confirm:

  • $20,000 USD target is reached or another safe funding path is confirmed.
  • Funds are actually accessible, not only estimated.
  • Land purchase route is practical.
  • Privacy plan for filming land content is ready.
  • The audience has been prepared for the transition.
