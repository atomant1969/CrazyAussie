Before Recording

  • Choose one target viewer.
  • Choose one clear video promise.
  • Pick the title idea before filming.
  • Pick the thumbnail concept before filming.
  • Decide whether this video is search-led, tutorial-led, review-led, or story-led.
  • Decide the next video this one should lead into.
  • Write the opening hook.
  • List required screen captures or footage.
  • List required audio checks.
  • Decide whether the video needs a Siberia bridge.
  • Check that no private information will appear in screen recordings.
  • Put phone and PC notifications on silent before recording.

Recording

  • Record the hook.
  • Record the main tutorial/review.
  • Record the closing bridge or call to action.
  • Capture any screen recordings.
  • Capture any cutaway shots.
  • Capture one possible Short.
  • Capture one possible thumbnail frame.
  • Record a clean version of any important step that must be readable.
  • Record room tone or quiet audio sample if audio cleanup will be demonstrated.
  • Check one test clip before recording the full video.

Editing

  • Remove dead space.
  • Make the first 30 seconds clear.
  • Keep the tutorial steps in order.
  • Add step labels if the tutorial has several stages.
  • Make screen text readable.
  • Balance voice and music.
  • Add basic captions where useful.
  • Blur private information if any appears.
  • Watch the full video before export.
  • Export at the correct resolution.
  • Export one or more Shorts if possible.
  • Watch the exported file before upload.

Packaging

  • Final title.
  • Put the most important words near the start of the title.
  • Choose auto-generated thumbnail frame or custom thumbnail.
  • Upload custom thumbnail if available.
  • Check thumbnail is readable when small.
  • Confirm thumbnail matches the title.
  • Description.
  • First two description lines clearly explain the video.
  • Chapters if useful.
  • Tags if useful.
  • Playlist if relevant.
  • Audience setting.
  • Visibility: public, private, unlisted, or scheduled.
  • Pinned comment.
  • End screen.
  • Link to related video or playlist.
  • Confirm no private location, account, file path, or personal information is visible.

After Publishing

  • Check video after upload.
  • Check title and thumbnail on mobile.
  • Check how the thumbnail appears in the video list.
  • Check description links.
  • Check chapters.
  • Check pinned comment.
  • Check playlist placement.
  • Check end screen.
  • Check visibility is correct.
  • Reply to early comments.
  • Record 24-hour metrics.
  • Record 7-day metrics.
  • Save useful viewer questions.
  • Add follow-up ideas to Video Roadmap.
