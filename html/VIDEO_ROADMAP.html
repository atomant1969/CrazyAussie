Launch Strategy

Start with content that new YouTubers are already searching for: editing software reviews, editing tutorials, upload workflow, and beginner production guidance.

The channel should not feel like a generic editing channel. Each video should gradually reveal that the creator is preparing to document a real Siberian homestead project.

Every major video should also pass through:

  • Viral Video Strategy: hook, tension, title, thumbnail, and shareability check.
  • Distribution And Promotion Plan: Shorts, TikTok, X.com, Pinterest, Facebook, and external promotion workflow.

Minimum Android Phone Specs

Recommended baseline for beginners:

  • 1080p video recording.
  • 30 fps minimum, 60 fps preferred.
  • 64 GB storage minimum, 128 GB preferred.
  • USB-C file transfer.
  • Enough battery to record multiple short takes.
  • Rear camera that can lock focus and exposure.
  • Clean lens, stable tripod mount, and enough storage space matter more than having the newest phone.
